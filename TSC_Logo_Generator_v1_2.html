Fonts, spacing, and use

The editable main mark uses Graduate, the same collegiate font used in the existing BSC designs. Main title lines use Red Rose Bold; the optional small line uses Red Rose Regular. All exported lettering is converted to paths; no font installation is needed.

The official TSC mark is preserved as supplied artwork. It is not a font or a complete editable alphabet. Selecting it locks the main mark while leaving the program title editable.

Automatic treatment enlarges a single line, following the Library examples. Large first line / smaller second follows the institutional hierarchy. A third line can appear above or below, in gold or blue for RGB output. Solid-color exports recolor every line consistently. Custom sizes are percentages of the standard title size.

Stacked lines are centered by their visible edges. Wide title lines share a visible left edge, and the complete block, including the small line, is vertically centered to the divider. Top and bottom clear space are equal. Long titles expand the canvas; very long main marks shrink uniformly to fit.

These are branding concepts, not an approval tool. Obtain TSC communications approval before official or outward-facing use. RGB colors are intended for digital use; this generator does not provide CMYK or spot-color separations.
